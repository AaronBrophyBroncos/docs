## What Makes GridIron GM Different

GridIron GM is built around three pillars that separate it from every other football simulation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Living Players">
    Every player is driven by a unique Persona DNA Matrix of 65+ traits. Their priorities shift week-to-week based on your team's performance — a Ring Chaser can become a Bag Chaser if your win rate collapses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Real Negotiation">
    Flat menus are gone. Contract talks happen through a live dialogue engine with Interest and Patience meters that react to every offer you make. One bad lowball offer can freeze negotiations for 48 in-game hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adaptive Gameplay">
    The AI defense reads your play-calling patterns and adjusts coverage mid-game. Weather evolves in real time, affecting ball physics and traction. No two games play the same.
  </Step>
</Steps>
