GridIron GM puts you in the seat of an NFL franchise decision-maker where every choice ripples through your roster, your locker room, and your dynasty. Unlike surface-level football games, GridIron GM models each player as an individual with evolving motivations, tracks the health of every negotiation in real time, and responds dynamically to how you play — rewarding creativity and punishing complacency. Whether you're engineering a 10-year dynasty in Franchise Mode, living the career of a rising superstar, or building a dream squad in Ultimate Team, every system in GridIron GM is designed to make you feel the weight of the NFL.

## The Three Pillars

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Living Players" icon="dna">
    The **Persona DNA Matrix** assigns every player 65+ behavioral traits that determine how they respond to contract offers, playing time, winning streaks, and locker-room dynamics. A Ring Chaser who watches your win percentage crater below .350 by Week 8 doesn't stay a Ring Chaser — he becomes a Bag Chaser, and his demands change with him.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real Negotiation" icon="handshake">
    The **Interactive Contract Engine** turns every free-agent meeting into a dialogue. Watch the Interest and Patience meters shift as you float offers. Lowball a player and his Patience drains; push too far and negotiations lock for 48 in-game hours while rival teams move in. Every conversation has stakes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adaptive Gameplay" icon="gamepad">
    **Smart Zone AI** reads your play-calling tendencies and adjusts coverage when you over-repeat a scheme. The right-stick tackle system rewards timing and angle. A dynamic weather engine turns a late-November home game into a strategic puzzle. The game learns how you play — and makes you work for every win.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Game Modes at a Glance

GridIron GM ships with four distinct modes, each built around a different relationship with the game:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Franchise Mode" icon="building">
    Full front-office control. Manage the draft, free agency, trades, contracts, coaching staff, and stadium finances across as many seasons as your dynasty demands.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Superstar Mode" icon="user">
    Choose a position, enter the draft, and live the career of a single player. Your on-field performance and off-field decisions shape your legacy and eventual Hall of Fame case. Progress through two distinct skill-tree paths: **Media Presence** (endorsements, fanbase growth, and contract leverage) or **Film Room Preparation** (technique ratings, pre-snap read speed, and situational IQ).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ultimate Team (MUT)" icon="cards">
    Build a squad from player cards earned through play, challenges, and the auction house. Upgrade cards permanently through the five-tier **MUT Evolution** system (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Elite → Icon). Compete in the **salary-cap H2H Weekend League** — a Friday-through-Sunday competitive ladder where every roster must fit under a shared cap ceiling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coach Mode" icon="clipboard">
    Call every play, manage every substitution, and control every strategy — but let the CPU handle on-field execution. Pure playcalling, zero stick skills required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Feature Highlights

| Feature | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Persona DNA Matrix** | 65+ traits per player; weights shift dynamically based on team performance and in-season events |
| **Decision Value Formula** | Every player evaluates offers using **w1(AAV) + w2(Guaranteed%) + w3(MarketSize) + w4(SnapShare) + w5(ChampionshipContenderIndex)** — weights shift as personas evolve |
| **Free Agency Bidding Clock** | 12-hour windows with simultaneous AI front-office competition and Franchise Tag options (Exclusive, Non-Exclusive, Transition) |
| **Holdout System** | Four-stage escalation: Locker Room Rumor → Social Media Cryptic Post → Pre-Season Holdout (fines accrue) → Formal Trade Request |
| **SuperSim Engine** | Simmed games resolve through individual positional matchups — not flat team overalls |
| **Right-Stick Tackle System** | Flick up for high tackles, down for low wraps, left/right for angle correction |
| **Smart Zone AI** | AI Adaptability Adjustment = Base Awareness × (Concept Calls × 0.15); coverage shells disguise pre-snap to post-snap |
| **Weather Physics Chain** | Dry Turf → Light Rain (+10% fumble rate) → Mud/Slippery (-20% cut traction); wind disrupts kicks, snow limits visibility, dome stadiums immune |
| **Broadcast Presentation** | Halftime shows, dynamic commentary, and career milestone tracking deliver a TV-quality experience |

<Note>
  GridIron GM uses a single save slot per mode by default. If you want to run parallel franchises or experiment with different teams, enable **Multi-Franchise Storage** in Settings → Save Data before starting your second file.
</Note>
