GridIron GM ships with five distinct modes, each built around a fundamentally different relationship with football. Franchise Mode puts you in complete control of a 32-man front office. Superstar Mode narrows the lens to one career, one snap at a time. Ultimate Team rewards strategic squad-building and card collection. Coach Mode strips away stick skills to deliver pure playcalling depth. Head-to-Head lets you test your franchise roster or dream squad directly against other players online. Every mode shares the same underlying simulation engine, which means the Persona DNA Matrix, SuperSim matchup logic, and dynamic weather system are active regardless of where you play.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Franchise Mode">
    Franchise Mode is GridIron GM's deepest offering — a full NFL franchise simulation where you manage every layer of an organization across as many seasons as your dynasty demands. You control personnel decisions, contract negotiations, the NFL Draft, trade deadlines, coaching hires, and (in Owner Mode) stadium finances and revenue strategy.

    **What makes it distinct:** Franchise Mode is the only mode where the Persona DNA Matrix fully activates at the roster level. Every player on your 53-man roster carries live trait weights that shift based on your win percentage, their snap share, their contract status, and league-wide events. A Ring Chaser on a team that falls below .350 by Week 8 begins transitioning toward Bag Chaser behavior — and his next contract demand will reflect it.

    **Key Features:**
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Full Contract Engine" icon="file-signature">
        Negotiate every deal through the interactive dialogue engine. Interest and Patience meters track each conversation. Franchise Tags (Exclusive, Non-Exclusive, Transition) give you leverage on your most critical players.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Free Agency Bidding" icon="gavel">
        12-hour bidding windows pit you against AI front offices in real time. UDFA signing periods and 90-to-53-man roster cutdowns add pressure every preseason.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Holdout System" icon="megaphone">
        Player dissatisfaction escalates through four stages: Locker Room Rumor → Social Media Cryptic Post → Pre-Season Holdout with Fine Accrual → Formal Trade Request. Early intervention is cheaper than late-stage damage control.
      </Card>
      <Card title="SuperSim Engine" icon="chart-bar">
        Simmed games resolve through individual positional matchups — your pass rusher's move rating vs. their tackle's anchor score — rather than flat team overalls. Every roster decision affects simulated outcomes.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    **How to access:** Main Menu → **Franchise Mode** → Start New Franchise or Continue Franchise.

    <Tip>
      Check the **Persona Snapshot** in your Roster Hub before every trade deadline. Selling a Ring Chaser who's watched your win% fall all season often returns more value than keeping a disgruntled player who's quietly started posting cryptic social media content.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Superstar Mode">
    Superstar Mode puts you inside the cleats of a single NFL player from draft night through the twilight of a career. You choose a position, go through the Combine and pre-draft process, get selected (or go undrafted and fight for a roster spot), and then play every snap of every game that involves your player.

    **What makes it distinct:** You experience the Persona DNA Matrix from the inside. Your own trait weights — Competitive Fire, Loyalty, Market Awareness, Ring Chaser vs. Bag Chaser tendencies — evolve based on your in-game decisions and performance. Whether you re-sign with a team that loves you or chase a bigger contract in free agency shapes your legacy score and eventual Hall of Fame case.

    **Key Features:**
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Career Arc System" icon="chart-line">
        Your development path branches based on on-field performance, media responses, training choices, and contract decisions. Breakout seasons unlock Superstar X-Factor abilities; declining play triggers regression events.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Off-Field Decisions" icon="microphone">
        Navigate press conferences, endorsement opportunities, and locker-room relationships. How you handle adversity and success shapes your Persona traits and your team's perception of you.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Legacy Score" icon="trophy">
        A running tally of career achievements, team contributions, and personal milestones. Your Legacy Score determines your Hall of Fame eligibility tier and the depth of your in-game retrospective.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Position-Specific Gameplay" icon="football">
        Each position has tailored mechanics — QBs manage pressure pockets and read progressions; pass rushers use the right-stick move system; receivers time catching windows and route-running cuts.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    **How to access:** Main Menu → **Superstar Mode** → Create Superstar.

    <Note>
      You can import a Superstar into a connected Franchise Mode save, allowing a friend (or second controller) to play as a star player on your franchise roster simultaneously. Enable **Connected Franchise** in the mode settings before starting either save.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Ultimate Team (MUT)">
    Ultimate Team challenges you to build the best squad possible from player cards earned through gameplay, challenges, and the auction house — then compete with that squad online or in solo content. MUT is the most card-driven mode in GridIron GM, but the on-field simulation engine is identical to every other mode.

    **What makes it distinct:** MUT introduces **Chemistry Bonuses** — groupings of players from the same real-world team, era, or archetype that unlock flat rating boosts when fielded together. Balancing raw card ratings against chemistry synergies is the central strategic puzzle of the mode.

    **Key Features:**
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Card Acquisition" icon="cards">
        Earn player packs through MUT Challenges, Season Objectives, and Daily Login rewards. Buy and sell individual cards on the **Auction House**, or complete **Sets** to exchange lower-rated cards for elite items.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Chemistry System" icon="flask">
        Build team chemistry by combining cards that share a Team, Theme, or Archetype tag. Chemistry Bonuses range from +2 Speed for three cards of the same archetype to full Superstar X-Factor unlocks for completing a legendary lineup.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Solo Challenges" icon="user">
        Hundreds of scenario-based challenges let you earn coins and packs without going online. Challenges range from historical game recreations to weekly live content tied to real NFL results.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Online Squads & Seasons" icon="globe">
        Compete in head-to-head seasons to climb division tiers and earn end-of-season rewards. **MUT Champions Weekend League** runs Friday through Sunday for the most competitive ladder.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    **How to access:** Main Menu → **Ultimate Team**.

    <Warning>
      MUT progress, cards, and coins are tied to your GridIron GM account and do not transfer between platforms. If you switch consoles mid-season, your MUT collection stays with your account but requires re-linking your platform profile in Account Settings.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Coach Mode">
    Coach Mode is for players who want the full strategic depth of GridIron GM without managing stick-skill execution. You call every play, manage every substitution, control clock management, and set all pre-snap adjustments — then watch the CPU execute your decisions with full simulation fidelity.

    **What makes it distinct:** The Smart Zone AI repetition penalty system is fully active in Coach Mode. Call the same concept too often in a drive and the CPU defense adjusts just as it would against a human player. Your playcalling intelligence determines the outcome, not your controller reaction time.

    **Key Features:**
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Full Playcall Authority" icon="clipboard">
        Access your full offensive and defensive playbooks on every snap. Use hot routes, motion, and pre-snap shifts before every play. The CPU executes — you strategize.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Smart Zone AI Interaction" icon="brain">
        Opposing AI tracks your play-call tendencies and adjusts mid-game. Diversify your concepts each series or face increasingly accurate pre-snap coverage reads from the defense.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Timeout & Clock Management" icon="clock">
        Call timeouts, manage the two-minute drill, and make fourth-down decisions manually. All game-management decisions remain in your hands.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Compatible with Franchise" icon="link">
        Run any Franchise Mode game in Coach Mode. The coaching style you use on the field feeds back into your staff reputation and scheme ratings over time.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    **How to access:** Before any game in Franchise Mode or a standalone exhibition, select **Coach Mode** from the Game Settings screen. You can also set Coach Mode as your default in Settings → Gameplay → Input Mode.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Head-to-Head">
    Head-to-Head lets you bring your Franchise roster or a custom squad directly into competition against other players online or in local multiplayer. It's the fastest path to competitive play in GridIron GM — no mode setup required, just pick your team and play.

    **What makes it distinct:** Head-to-Head uses the same full gameplay engine as every other mode — right-stick tackle system, timed catching windows, Smart Zone AI, and dynamic weather — meaning the skills you build in Franchise or Superstar Mode transfer directly.

    **Key Features:**
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Ranked Seasons" icon="medal">
        Compete in 10-game ranked seasons to earn division placements and end-of-season cosmetic rewards. Matchmaking uses an ELO-based system that accounts for team overall ratings to ensure competitive parity.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Franchise Export" icon="upload">
        Export your current Franchise Mode roster — including any developed players and earned X-Factor abilities — into a Head-to-Head lobby. Show off the team you built.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Local Multiplayer" icon="gamepad">
        Play split-screen or same-console H2H with a second controller. All gameplay modes and difficulty settings are available in local play.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Weekly Scenarios" icon="calendar">
        Each week, GridIron GM publishes curated H2H scenarios tied to that week's real NFL matchups — specific rosters, weather conditions, and situational rules that mirror the actual game.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    **How to access:** Main Menu → **Head-to-Head** → Quick Match, Ranked, or Local Multiplayer.

    <Tip>
      Use **Practice Mode** (accessible from the Head-to-Head lobby) to drill your right-stick tackle timing and catching windows before entering ranked play. Practice Mode lets you run any play from any formation against a controlled defense with instant replay feedback.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Which Mode Is Right for You?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Dynasty Builder" icon="crown">
    **Best Mode: Franchise Mode**

    You want to engineer a multi-year NFL powerhouse, manage a salary cap, master the draft, and watch your personnel decisions pay off over a decade of seasons.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Story-Driven Player" icon="user-astronaut">
    **Best Mode: Superstar Mode**

    You want a personal narrative — one player's journey from the draft to Canton, shaped by your decisions on and off the field.
  </Card>

    **Best Mode: Ultimate Team**

    You love building and optimizing squads from a constantly evolving card pool, chasing elite items, and competing in weekly live content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pure Strategist" icon="chess">
    **Best Mode: Coach Mode**

    You want to outthink the opponent at the playcall level without worrying about controller execution. Schematic mastery is the entire game.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Competitive Player" icon="swords">
    **Best Mode: Head-to-Head**

    You want to test your skills against real opponents immediately, whether that's a ranked climb, a Franchise export showcase, or a couch rivalry.

  </Card>

## Shared Systems Across All Modes

Regardless of which mode you play, these core systems are always running under the hood:

<Accordion title="SuperSim Engine">
  Every simulated game — whether you're skipping preseason in Franchise Mode or watching a MUT Solo Challenge resolve — uses the **SuperSim Engine**, which processes individual positional matchups rather than team-level averages. Your wide receiver's release rating is tested against the cornerback's jam technique on every simulated route. Roster quality matters in sim results.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Dynamic Weather Engine">
  Weather conditions are generated from realistic regional and seasonal data for each stadium. Rain reduces footing and increases fumble probability. Wind affects deep-ball accuracy and field goal range. Snow accumulates over game quarters and progressively slows player speed. All weather effects apply in live play and SuperSim equally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Broadcast Presentation">
  Every mode benefits from GridIron GM's broadcast-quality presentation layer: dynamically scripted halftime shows that reference your season's storylines, a commentary system that tracks career milestones and calls them out in real time, and camera packages that mirror real NFL broadcast angles. The presentation layer updates weekly with real-world references in modes connected to live content (MUT, H2H Weekly Scenarios).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Right-Stick Tackle System">
  The right-stick tackle system is available in any mode where you control a defensive player on the field. Flick up for a high tackle targeting the ball carrier's chest and shoulders, flick down for a low wrap targeting the legs, and flick left or right to correct your pursuit angle mid-tackle animation. Mistimed flicks result in missed tackles; well-timed flicks at the correct angle unlock tackle-for-loss and strip-ball opportunities.
</Accordion>
