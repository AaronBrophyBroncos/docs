Superstar Mode lets you step away from the front office and onto the field as an individual NFL player. Instead of managing rosters and contracts, you control every snap, milestone, and off-field decision for a single superstar — building a legacy defined by both your statistical dominance and the kind of player you choose to become. Every touchdown, press conference, and film session feeds directly into your skill tree, making each career feel genuinely distinct.

## Persona Paths

Your superstar's development is shaped by two overarching persona philosophies. You choose where to invest your off-field time, and those choices unlock different branches of your skill tree, alter how teammates and coaches respond to you, and determine which endorsement and coaching opportunities become available.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Media Presence">
    The Media Presence path turns your superstar into a cultural force — a player whose brand is as powerful as their on-field performance. Lean into press appearances, social media engagement, and public-facing events to build a persona that elevates the entire franchise.

    **What Media Presence unlocks:**

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Marketing Attribute Boosts" icon="bullhorn">
        Increase your jersey sales rating, fan engagement score, and sponsorship appeal — attributes that drive front-office revenue and team morale bonuses.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Team Morale Impact" icon="users">
        Your public-facing confidence radiates through the locker room. High Media Presence ratings provide passive morale boosts to teammates after big wins and media moments.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Endorsement Opportunities" icon="star">
        Unlock exclusive brand deals that generate off-field income and award bonus skill points you can invest back into your on-field attributes.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Signature Entrance Sequences" icon="camera">
        Earn custom stadium entrance animations and celebration packages tied to your public persona tier.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Tip>
      Media Presence synergizes well with positions that already generate public attention — QBs, WRs, and CBs benefit most from the morale and endorsement bonuses.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Film Room Preparation">
    The Film Room Preparation path turns your superstar into a cerebral, technically elite player. Invest your off-field time studying opponents, refining mechanics, and mastering your playbook to unlock advanced football IQ nodes that purely physical players can never access.

    **What Film Room Preparation unlocks:**

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Football IQ Boosts" icon="brain">
        Raise your awareness, play recognition, and pursuit angle ratings — the hidden attributes that separate good players from shutdown ones in the SuperSim engine.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Route Recognition" icon="route">
        WRs and TEs unlock the ability to read coverage pre-snap and subtly adjust route timing, increasing separation in simmed and user-controlled plays alike.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Pre-Snap Read Upgrades" icon="eye">
        QBs gain access to advanced protection audibles and hot-route packages unavailable to players who skip Film Room progression.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Advanced Play-Call Audibles" icon="clipboard-list">
        Unlock a deeper audible menu at the line of scrimmage, letting you counter defensive alignments that would otherwise neutralize your base play design.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Tip>
      Film Room is the stronger path for positions where intelligence compounds physical ability — QBs, ILBs, and safeties see the biggest simulation-side gains.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  You don't have to commit entirely to one path. Splitting your off-field investments roughly 60/40 toward your primary persona while dipping into the other unlocks a balanced set of nodes — especially useful mid-career when your on-field stats plateau and you need secondary attribute boosts to stay elite.
</Tip>

## Skill Tree Progression

Your skill tree advances through a combination of **on-field milestone completions** and **persona point investments**. Every node you unlock delivers one or more of the following:

- A permanent attribute boost to a specific rating
- A new in-game animation (catch style, pass release, pass-rush move)
- A special ability that activates under defined in-game conditions

### How On-Field Goals Feed the Tree

At the start of each season, you receive a set of position-specific milestone targets. Hitting these targets awards skill points you spend directly in your tree. Miss a target and the points don't appear — consistent performers unlock nodes faster than inconsistent ones.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Your Season Milestone Targets">
    Open the Career Hub at the start of each season to see your milestone slate. Targets scale with your current overall rating — an 82 OVR player faces achievable but challenging benchmarks, not the same bar as a 95 OVR veteran.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn Skill Points Through Gameplay">
    Milestone completions award skill points in real time. Hit a receiving yards threshold mid-season and the points appear in your tree immediately — you don't have to wait until the offseason to invest them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invest Points in Your Skill Tree">
    Navigate to the Skill Tree in the Career Hub. Each node shows its cost, the attribute or ability it grants, and any prerequisite nodes that must be unlocked first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock Animations and Special Abilities">
    Certain high-cost nodes deep in the tree are gated behind both skill point requirements and milestone completions. Reaching them requires sustained performance across multiple seasons, not a single breakout game.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Importing Coaching Philosophy from College Dynasty

If you've built a program in College Dynasty mode, you can carry its offensive or defensive philosophy directly into your Superstar Mode career at draft time.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Offensive Philosophy Import" icon="football">
    Importing a spread or air-raid philosophy from College Dynasty gives your QB or WR superstar a head start on Film Room progression nodes tied to route concepts and pass timing that match the imported scheme.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Defensive Philosophy Import" icon="shield">
    A pressure-heavy or Cover-2 shell philosophy imported for a defensive superstar pre-unlocks scheme-specific awareness nodes and grants a one-time bonus to position-relevant recognition attributes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Philosophy imports are a one-time selection made during the pre-draft setup screen. You can't swap philosophies mid-career, so review your College Dynasty scheme before committing — it shapes which early skill tree nodes are discounted or pre-unlocked.
</Note>
