MUT Evolution replaces the luck of the pack-pull with a system built around measurable effort. Every player card you own can now be upgraded permanently by completing a series of position-specific milestone objectives — no duplicate card combinations, no randomized outcomes. Finish the milestones, upgrade the card, and keep that upgrade forever. The result is a squad-building path that rewards consistent play rather than credit card spending.

## Evolution Tiers

Every player card exists on a five-tier evolution ladder. Upgrading through each tier raises the card's overall rating, unlocks new attributes, and changes the card's visual appearance to reflect its new tier.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Bronze" icon="medal">
    Starting tier for all Evolution-eligible cards. Bronze cards carry base ratings pulled from real-world performance data. Completing Bronze milestones opens the Silver upgrade path.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Silver" icon="medal">
    The first meaningful ratings jump. Silver unlocks secondary attributes not present on the Bronze version and introduces position-specific ability slots.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gold" icon="medal">
    Gold cards become genuine squad contributors. Ratings reach competitive levels and the card's first signature ability slot becomes available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Elite" icon="star">
    Elite-tier cards can anchor your starting lineup. A second ability slot opens and the card's chemistry tags expand, giving you more squad-building flexibility.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Icon" icon="trophy">
    The pinnacle of Evolution. Icon cards represent peak-career versions of their players, carry maximum attribute ceilings, and unlock exclusive Icon-tier animations and abilities unavailable at any lower tier.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Every tier upgrade is **permanent**. Once you complete the milestone set and confirm the upgrade, the card is locked at its new tier and all earned attribute gains are retained — they cannot be reverted, removed, or traded away mid-progression. This is a deliberate departure from temporary boost tokens, which expire. What you earn, you keep.
</Note>

## Milestone System

Each position has its own milestone track tailored to the stats and situations that actually define excellence at that spot. Milestones are grouped into tiers — completing a full tier's milestone set triggers the card upgrade to the next level.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Quarterback">
    <Accordion title="Bronze → Silver Milestones">
      - Pass for 800 yards across any combination of games
      - Complete 65% or more of your passes in a single game
      - Throw 5 touchdowns with zero interceptions in a game
      - Win 2 games with a passer rating above 100
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Silver → Gold Milestones">
      - Pass for 2,500 yards in a single season
      - Maintain a TD-to-INT ratio of 3:1 or better over a 6-game stretch
      - Complete 3 fourth-quarter comeback drives
      - Record a perfect passer rating (158.3) in any game
    </Accordion>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Wide Receiver">
    <Accordion title="Bronze → Silver Milestones">
      - Record 400 receiving yards across any combination of games
      - Win 3 contested catch situations in a single game
      - Record a 100+ yard receiving game
      - Score 4 receiving touchdowns total
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Silver → Gold Milestones">
      - Record 1,000 receiving yards in a single season
      - Win 15 contested catches over a season
      - Post 5 games with 80+ receiving yards
      - Record a receiving touchdown of 40+ yards
    </Accordion>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Defensive Line / Edge">
    <Accordion title="Bronze → Silver Milestones">
      - Record 5 sacks across any combination of games
      - Force 2 fumbles total
      - Record 10 quarterback hits
      - Win 60% of your pass-rush rep matchups in a single game
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Silver → Gold Milestones">
      - Record 10 sacks in a single season
      - Force 4 fumbles over a season
      - Record 3 sacks in a single game
      - Achieve a pass-rush win rate above 70% for 3 consecutive games
    </Accordion>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to Evolve a Card: Bronze to Silver

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Your Evolution Card">
    Open the MUT Roster screen and filter by "Evolution Eligible." Tap any Bronze-tier card to open its Evolution Profile, which shows the full milestone list, your current progress on each, and the projected Silver card ratings once the upgrade is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Card to Your Active Lineup">
    Milestone progress only tracks when the card is in your active lineup and actually participates in eligible game modes. Bench players don't accumulate milestone credit — get the card on the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete All Bronze Milestone Objectives">
    Work through your position-specific milestone list. Progress is tracked automatically after every game. You can partial-complete milestones across multiple sessions — nothing resets between play sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Tier Upgrade">
    Once all Bronze milestones show a completion checkmark, a glowing "Upgrade Available" banner appears on the card. Open the Evolution Profile and select **Confirm Upgrade**. The card immediately transforms to its Silver version with updated art, ratings, and ability slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin the Silver Milestone Track">
    Your newly upgraded Silver card automatically loads its next milestone set. The process repeats through Gold, Elite, and ultimately Icon tier — each set progressively more demanding to reflect the card's rising ceiling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Salary-Cap-Restricted H2H Weekend League

The Weekend League is MUT Evolution's competitive head-to-head mode. It runs Thursday through Sunday each week with a hard salary cap enforced on every squad, ensuring that raw card spending doesn't simply determine match outcomes.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="How the Cap Works" icon="scale-balanced">
    Before entering the Weekend League, the system calculates the total market value of every card in your submitted lineup. If your squad's combined value exceeds the weekly cap threshold, you must make substitutions until you fall within the limit. No exceptions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cap Resets Weekly" icon="rotate">
    The salary cap threshold is recalibrated each week based on the current card market. An Icon card that's relatively affordable one week may push squads over the cap the next if market values shift — staying cap-compliant is an ongoing roster management challenge.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evolution Cards and Cap Value" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Evolved cards are valued at their **current tier**, not their Bronze starting point. A player you've ground to Elite tier carries Elite-tier cap value. Strong Evolution cards can absolutely fill a Weekend League roster — but their earned ratings count against the cap just like purchased cards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rewards Structure" icon="gift">
    Weekend League rewards scale with your win total across the event window. Top finishes award high-tier Evolution milestone packs, exclusive cosmetic items, and bonus skill points usable in Superstar Mode careers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  The cap is enforced at **squad submission**, not at the start of each individual match. If you swap in a card between games that pushes your squad over the threshold, you'll receive an immediate cap violation alert and must resolve the conflict before your next match timer begins. Unresolved violations result in a forfeit for that scheduled match — not a suspension, but a lost win opportunity you can't recover within that weekend's window.
</Warning>

## Evolution vs. Traditional Set Collecting

MUT Evolution and the legacy set-collecting system coexist — you don't have to choose one exclusively.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set Collecting" icon="layer-group">
    Trade, collect, and combine cards to complete themed sets for pack rewards and high-tier card unlocks. Best for players who enjoy the collection and trading side of MUT and want the fastest path to top-rated cards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Evolution System" icon="chart-line">
    Earn permanent upgrades through gameplay milestones. Slower path to elite card ratings, but every gain is yours to keep and the progression is entirely skill- and effort-driven. Best for players who want long-term investment in specific cards.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Use set collecting to quickly fill positional gaps at the start of a Weekend League season, then focus your Evolution grind on the two or three cards you want to carry as cornerstones for the long term. Trying to Evolution-grind an entire starting lineup simultaneously spreads your game time too thin to hit milestone deadlines efficiently.
</Tip>
