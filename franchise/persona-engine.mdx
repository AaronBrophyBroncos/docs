Every player in your franchise carries a living psychological profile built from a primary archetype and a constellation of sub-traits drawn from a pool of 65 distinct characteristics. These traits are not cosmetic labels — they actively govern how a player responds to contract offers, reacts to trade rumors, performs under pressure, and influences the locker room around him. Understanding the persona engine is the difference between a front office that reacts to drama and one that anticipates it before it starts.

## The Decision Value Formula

When a player evaluates any major franchise decision — signing an extension, accepting a trade, or tolerating a reduced role — the game calculates a **Decision Value** score under the hood. The higher the score relative to competing offers or situations, the more likely the player is to act favorably toward your franchise.

```
Decision Value = w1(AAV) + w2(Guaranteed%) + w3(MarketSize) + w4(SnapShare) + w5(ChampionshipContenderIndex)
```

Each variable represents a weighted input:

| Variable | What It Measures | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `AAV` | Average Annual Value of the contract offer | $22M/yr scores higher than $18M/yr |
| `Guaranteed%` | Percentage of the deal that is fully guaranteed | 60% guaranteed outweighs 30% on equal AAV |
| `MarketSize` | Franchise market tier (1–5 scale, 5 = largest) | New York scores 5; Green Bay scores 2 |
| `SnapShare` | Projected snaps as a percentage of team total | A WR2 role at 55% share vs. a WR1 at 80% |
| `ChampionshipContenderIndex` | Team's computed playoff and Super Bowl probability | Ranges 0.0–1.0; above 0.70 = contender |

### Dynamic Weight Shifting

The weights `w1` through `w5` are not static. They recalibrate every week based on in-game outcomes — wins, losses, injuries, and off-field events. A player who entered Week 1 prioritizing championships may have shifted his formula by Week 10 based on your team's trajectory.

<Note>
  Monitor each player's active trait weights in the **Franchise Hub → Player Profile → Persona Tab**. The radar chart there updates weekly and highlights any weights that have shifted by more than 15% since the previous check, flagging potential attitude changes before they escalate into holdouts or trade demands.
</Note>

## Archetypes and Primary Motivations

Every player is assigned one **primary archetype** at the time of draft class generation or free agent pool creation. The archetype sets the default distribution of `w1`–`w5` weights and determines which sub-traits are most likely to appear in that player's profile.

| Archetype | Primary Driver | Key Weight Bias | Locker Room Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ring Chaser** | Championships above all else | `w5` (Championship Index) highest | Vocal leader, rallies team in losses |
| **Bag Chaser** | Maximum contract value | `w1` (AAV) + `w2` (Guaranteed%) dominant | Neutral; disengages if underpaid |
| **Hometown Hero** | Playing in home market | `w3` (MarketSize) skewed to home city | Community ambassador, fan favorite |
| **Media Magnet** | Exposure and brand growth | `w3` (MarketSize) + social trait cluster | Press-room presence, polarizing internally |
| **Film Room Rat** | Role clarity and scheme fit | `w4` (SnapShare) + scheme-fit trait cluster | Quiet leader, mentors younger players |
| **Legacy Builder** | Career statistical milestones | Balanced across `w1`, `w4`, `w5` | Focused; dislikes disorganized depth charts |
| **Alpha Dog** | Clear WR1 / starter designation | `w4` (SnapShare) dominant | Friction with crowded position rooms |
| **Journeyman** | Stability and job security | `w2` (Guaranteed%) dominant | Low morale footprint, easy to manage |

Sub-traits from the 65-trait pool layer onto the archetype. For example, a Ring Chaser with the **Injury Aware** sub-trait will still prioritize championships but will place abnormally high value on guaranteed money as a secondary concern — because he has seen teammates' careers cut short.

## Dynamic Trait Shifting

Archetypes are not permanent. Extended poor performance, roster upheaval, or a sustained losing environment can cause a player's primary archetype to shift, bringing a new weight distribution with it.

### Example: Ring Chaser → Bag Chaser

Consider a veteran wide receiver who enters your franchise as a **Ring Chaser** — he took a team-friendly deal in the offseason because your Championship Contender Index read 0.78. By Week 8 your team sits at 2–6, win percentage at .250 and trending down. The game evaluates the threshold condition:

```
IF archetype == "Ring Chaser"
AND team_win_pct < 0.350
AND current_week >= 8
THEN trigger_archetype_drift_check()
```

The drift check runs a weighted probability calculation factoring in the player's **Loyalty** sub-trait score and his remaining contract years. A player with low Loyalty and one year left converts immediately to **Bag Chaser**. A player with high Loyalty and three years remaining may resist the shift entirely — or convert more slowly, passing through an intermediate **Frustrated Ring Chaser** state that surfaces as increased holdout risk rather than an instant archetype flip.

Once the conversion completes, `w1` and `w2` become the dominant weights. He will no longer accept below-market extensions citing team vision. His negotiation Patience Meter will start shorter in future talks, and he may begin generating **Locker Room Rumor** events if his AAV sits below the position's market average.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Before Shift (Ring Chaser)">
    ```
    w1 (AAV)                  = 0.10
    w2 (Guaranteed%)          = 0.10
    w3 (MarketSize)           = 0.15
    w4 (SnapShare)            = 0.25
    w5 (ChampionshipIndex)    = 0.40
    ```
    This player accepted $3M below market because your team was a Super Bowl contender.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="After Shift (Bag Chaser)">
    ```
    w1 (AAV)                  = 0.40
    w2 (Guaranteed%)          = 0.35
    w3 (MarketSize)           = 0.10
    w4 (SnapShare)            = 0.10
    w5 (ChampionshipIndex)    = 0.05
    ```
    He now wants a market-rate extension immediately. The championship pitch no longer moves the needle.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sub-Traits Reference

Sub-traits modify how the base archetype behaves at the edges. Every player carries between three and seven active sub-traits at any given time. Some sub-traits are permanent; others can be gained or lost through in-season events.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Motivational Sub-Traits" icon="fire">
    Traits like **Clutch**, **Media Hungry**, and **Contract Year Beast** amplify performance or negotiation leverage at specific moments. A player with Contract Year Beast gains a temporary `w1` boost in the final year of his deal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Loyalty & Stability Sub-Traits" icon="shield">
    Traits like **Franchise Pillar**, **Deep Roots**, and **Low Maintenance** slow archetype drift and reduce holdout probability. These players are anchors — invest in them for long-term stability.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chemistry Sub-Traits" icon="users">
    **Locker Room Leader**, **Mentor**, and **Spark Plug** affect team-wide morale and younger players' development trajectories. Releasing a Locker Room Leader without a replacement can cause a cascade of morale decay.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Volatile Sub-Traits" icon="bolt">
    Traits like **Drama Magnet**, **Social Media Loose Cannon**, and **Agent Driven** increase the probability of escalation events. These players carry high upside but require active front-office management to keep stable.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Viewing and Tracking Traits

Open any player's full profile from the roster screen and navigate to the **Persona** tab. You will find:

- **Archetype Badge** with a drift probability indicator showing how stable the current archetype is
- **Weight Radar Chart** displaying current `w1`–`w5` distribution
- **Active Sub-Traits** list with tooltips explaining each trait's mechanical effect
- **Trait History Log** showing any shifts that have occurred during your franchise save
- **Mood Indicator** — a high-level read on the player's current satisfaction level across contract, role, and team performance dimensions

Use the **Franchise Hub → Persona Dashboard** to see a team-wide heat map of archetypes and identify clusters of volatile traits before they compound into a locker room crisis.
